Lead Scoring Guide
What is Lead Scoring?

Automatically rank contacts based on their engagement. High scores = hot leads ready to buy!

Why Score Leads?
  • Focus on hottest leads first
  • Save time on cold leads
  • Increase conversion rates
  • Better sales alignment
Scoring Criteria
  • Engagement: Opens, clicks, replies
  • Demographics: Job title, company size
  • Behavior: Website visits, purchases
Score Ranges
  • 0-30: Cold lead
  • 31-60: Warm lead
  • 61-100: Hot lead
  • 100+: Ready to buy!
