Campaign Guide
What is a Campaign?

A campaign is a single email sent to a group of contacts. Track performance in real-time.

Campaign Types
  • Newsletter - Regular updates
  • Promotional - Sales & offers
  • Announcement - Product launches
Best Practices
  • Send during business hours
  • Avoid spam trigger words
  • Always include unsubscribe link
  • Test on different devices
Metrics to Track
  • Open Rate - Subject line effectiveness
  • Click Rate - Content engagement
  • Bounce Rate - List quality
Marketing Tip: Send campaigns on Tuesday or Thursday mornings for highest engagement!
