Contact Management Guide
Why Clean Contacts Matter

Clean contact lists improve deliverability and engagement. Remove invalid emails regularly to maintain a healthy sender reputation.

Best Practices
  • Always get permission before adding contacts
  • Segment contacts by interests and behavior
  • Update contact information regularly
  • Remove inactive contacts periodically
Import Tips
  • Use CSV or Excel files
  • Map columns correctly
  • Check for duplicate emails
  • Verify data before importing
Engagement Tracking
  • Active - Opens and clicks emails
  • Unsubscribed - Opted out
  • Bounced - Invalid email address
