How to Use Lists Effectively
What are Lists?

Lists are groups of contacts organized by common characteristics. Think of them as folders for your contacts.

Why Use Lists?

Send targeted emails to specific groups. Better targeting = higher engagement = more sales!

Best Practices
  • Keep list names clear and descriptive
  • Segment by behavior (opens, clicks, purchases)
  • Clean lists regularly
Common List Ideas
  • Newsletter Subscribers - General updates
  • New Customers - Welcome series
  • VIP Customers - Special offers
  • Inactive Users - Re-engagement
Marketing Tip: Businesses using segmented lists see 760% increase in revenue compared to non-segmented campaigns!
