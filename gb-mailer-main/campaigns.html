Campaign Management Guide
What are Campaigns?

Campaigns are bulk email sends to targeted lists. They allow you to schedule, track, and analyze your email marketing efforts.

A/B Testing

Test two versions of your email to see which performs better. The system automatically sends the winner to more recipients.

Best Practices
  • Schedule for optimal times
  • Segment your audience
  • Monitor open/click rates
  • Clean bounced emails
Key Metrics
  • Open Rate - % who opened
  • Click Rate - % who clicked links
  • Bounce Rate - % undelivered
  • Unsubscribe - % opted out
